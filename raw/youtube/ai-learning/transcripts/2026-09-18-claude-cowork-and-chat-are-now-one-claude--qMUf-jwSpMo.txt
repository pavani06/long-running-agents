You know that half-second when you open your laptop to give Claude something...
And you hesitate and think, “Where do I get started?” I’m Meaghan Choi, and I lead design for Claude apps.
Rolling out today, you don’t have to pick between chat and Cowork anymore.
It’s all one conversation, and Claude brings in whatever the task needs. Cowork is where we worked out how Claude should take on bigger, multi-step work.
It got so good at deciding what a task actually needs, keeping it in a separate place just stopped making sense. You no longer have to figure out where a task belongs before you start.
And the capabilities of Cowork are now more accessible everywhere — your chats, tasks, skills, and memories are all still intact.
Claude will meet you where the work is. Ask it a question or give it a bigger task.
Or hand it last week’s numbers and have it draft the deck or other visuals, and it’ll build it all right there. It’s all the same conversation now.
The work comes back yours. You’ll still be able to keep, edit, or steer any piece you want. This is the first step.
As the models keep improving, we’ll keep improving the experience as well. We'll bring you along as we go. Try it, and let us know what you think.
